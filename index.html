The Prophetic Routine

A comprehensive guide to living with barakah, purpose & balance
Based on Seerah & Hadith
🕌

Core Philosophy

"If you are not being productive every day, every moment of your life, you are wasting the most valuable asset you have."

"The best routine is the routine of the best of mankind — the last prophet Muhammad ﷺ. No Bali, no folly. Just the Sunnah."

Sheikh — from transcript

The Prophetic routine rests on three uncompromising pillars:

🕌

Ibadah First

Structure your entire day around Salah — not the other way around. The five daily prayers are non-negotiable anchors that give the day its skeleton.

⚡

Eliminate Waste

Doom scrolling, binge-watching, excessive sleep, and idle entertainment drain the time that belongs to your deen and dunya. People say they have no time — they are lying to themselves.

⚖️

Balance, Not Extremism

The Prophet ﷺ raced with Aisha RA, joked with children, sat with family, and watched entertainment at Eid. Total austerity breeds rebellion. The Sunnah IS the balance.

"Shouldn't I be a thankful servant? Shukr is action — not just words on the tongue."

Prophet Muhammad ﷺ — Sahih Bukhari

✅ The Sunnah Approach

  • ◆Schedule deen time AND family time deliberately
  • ◆Bond with children — be friends with them
  • ◆Help spouse around the house out of goodness
  • ◆Allow halal entertainment within its place
  • ◆Stay flexible when the ummah needs you

❌ The Two Extremes

  • ◆Total harshness → children rebel from deen
  • ◆Total permissiveness → no structure, no barakah
  • ◆Using family as excuse to miss masjid
  • ◆Halal entertainment becoming 6-hr daily habit
  • ◆Praising celebrities instead of scholars to children

Prophetic Daily Schedule

Last ⅓ of night
~2–3 AM
Ibadah Wake before Fajr. Wudu, miswak. Begin Tahajjud: 11 rak'at in segments of 2+2+2+2+2+1. Recite Al-Baqarah, Aal-Imran, An-Nisa. Extended ruku & sujud. Long dua in sujud.
Pre-Adhan
Ibadah Wait for adhan sitting. Sahabah came with questions — he answered before Fajr. Dhikr, reflection.
Fajr
Ibadah Pray Fajr in jama'ah. After salah: turn to companions, interpret dreams, teach masa'il. Sit until Ishraq (15–20 min after sunrise). Pray Ishraq 2 rak'at.
Post-Ishraq
Family Return home. Help wife in house. Mend own clothes. Teach children & grandchildren. Give family their right in the morning hours.
Duha → Dhuhr
Productivity Prime Block 1. Ummah affairs, business, governance, dawah, teaching, resolving disputes. Mind is sharpest. Ideal for deep work.
Dhuhr
Ibadah Pray early — never delayed. Post-salah: turn to teach Sahabah, interpret issues, deal with community affairs.
Post-Dhuhr
Qailulah
Rest Qailulah — midday nap. 20 minutes to 1 hour. Essential for sustaining Qiyam al-Layl. In very hot seasons, Dhuhr was delayed to after the nap.
Post-Qailulah
→ 'Asr
Productivity Prime Block 2. Work, deen study, Quran revision, personal research. Run errands, attend to needs.
'Asr
Ibadah Pray early — he did not delay 'Asr. Post-salah: family time, errands, deen work. Use this window before Maghrib.
Maghrib
Ibadah Pray Maghrib immediately at time. Short post-salah dhikr. Brief family check-in before Isha window begins.
Maghrib → Isha
Family Family dinner, bonding, halal entertainment within limits. Sit with wife, sit with children. The Prophet ﷺ sat with Aisha RA and watched entertainment on Eid.
Isha
~9–10 PM
Ibadah Delay Isha to end of first third of night (never past midnight). Pray with congregation. At times stayed for critical ummah discussions — but this was the exception.
Post-Isha
Rest Go directly to sleep. He disliked speaking after Isha. Woke his wives and children for Tahajjud when he rose in the last third of the night.

Sleep Science & Sunnah

⏱ Optimal Duration

4–6
hours of night sleep + Qailulah
Night sleep (4–6 hrs)
Qailulah nap (20–60 min)
⚠ Avoid: sleeping after Fajr
Destroys the barakah of the entire day — avoid entirely

🕘 Best Sleep Window

Sleep after Isha (~9–10 PM) until the last third of the night (~2–3 AM).

"Early-night sleep gives 2× the restorative value of late-night sleep."

Sleep research — confirmed by circadian science
Slow-wave (deep) sleep is front-loaded in the night. The Prophet ﷺ inadvertently optimised both spiritual output and biological recovery simultaneously.

"O Allah, put barakah in the early mornings of my ummah."

Prophet Muhammad ﷺ — Hadith
📋

Sleep Rules

✅ Do This

  • ◆Sleep immediately after Isha — no idle talk
  • ◆Take Qailulah if doing Tahajjud — non-negotiable
  • ◆Wake family for Tahajjud when rising
  • ◆Stay awake after Fajr (the barakah window)

❌ Avoid This

  • ◆Sleeping after Fajr (kills the barakah of the day)
  • ◆More than 6–7 hrs (excessive, wasteful)
  • ◆Sleeping past the middle of the night without praying
  • ◆Screen use post-Isha (delays sleep onset)

Time Killers to Eliminate

"People say 'I don't have time.' They are lying. They don't use their time correctly."

❌ Hard Cuts — Eliminate Entirely

✕Doom scrolling — Reels, Shorts, TikTok
✕Netflix / YouTube binge-watching
✕Music, haram content
✕Sleeping post-Fajr
✕Sleeping > 6–7 hours without need
✕Idle, purposeless conversation after Isha

⚠️ Limit — Schedule, Don't Ban

△Sports watching (designated time only)
△Halal entertainment with family (Eid / off days)
△Social media (defined daily window)
△Video games with children (bonding, within limits)
△Nasheeds — halal but not very productive
△Errands and shopping (batch, don't wander)

"Doom scrolling has ruined our generation. People can't wait for a 30-second video to end — they're already bored. How will you learn Quran if you can't sustain attention?"

The Family Exception

The Prophet ﷺ sat with Aisha RA and watched acrobats perform at Eid. He raced her in the desert. He played with Hassan and Hussein during sujud. The caveat: this was scheduled, purposeful bonding — not daily 6-hour habits. When the adhan came, his face changed. He did not know them. The deen was never compromised for entertainment.

Productivity Principles

Click each principle to expand.

1
Schedule Around Salah — Not Vice Versa
▶
Place all five prayers into your calendar first. Everything else fills around them. The adhan is a live productivity alarm five times daily — a non-Muslim economics professor in Saudi Arabia once noted that his colleagues effectively had five structured calendar reminders on loudspeakers every single day. This is your natural time-management system built into the deen.
2
Guard the Fajr–Ishraq Window Fiercely
▶
This is the single most blessed time of the day. Allah's dua for barakah was specifically for the "early morning of the ummah." Your mind is rested, the world is quiet, cortisol is naturally elevated for focus. Use it for: Quran revision, memorisation, learning, and your deepest most important work. Even non-Muslim productivity experts universally recommend early mornings — they accidentally discovered what the Sunnah established 1400 years ago.
3
Qailulah Is Non-Negotiable for Night Worship
▶
The scholars confirmed it: if you want to consistently maintain Qiyam al-Layl, take a midday nap. Even 20–30 minutes resets sleep pressure and cortisol, enabling a second full productivity block in the afternoon AND the capacity to wake at 2–3 AM without agony. The Prophet ﷺ took this nap regularly. His Sahabah took it. This is how they managed to pray at night every single day of their lives.
4
Batch Activities in Natural Time Blocks
▶
The Prophet ﷺ worked in distinct, purposeful modes — not scattered throughout the day. Teaching happened after salah. Family time happened after returning from masjid. Work happened in the Duha and post-Asr blocks. Sleep happened immediately after Isha. Apply this: don't mix modes. Deep work time is not family time. Family time is not scrolling time. Each block has a purpose and an endpoint — that is what gives the day structure.
5
Maintain Family Time Without Compromising Deen
▶
He raced with Aisha RA in the desert. He prolonged sujud so Hussein RA could dismount his own neck. He joked with children. He interpreted the grief of a small boy whose bird died. But — when the adhan came, his face changed. He "did not know them." The deen is the priority, always. Family time is not sacrificed. But it never replaces or delays the obligations of the deen. Sheikh noted: being too harsh causes children to rebel. Being a friend to your children, playing games with them within limits, is itself the Sunnah.
6
Build Firm Structure, Then Flex for the Ummah
▶
There were nights the Prophet ﷺ stayed late after Isha discussing critical ummah matters with Abu Bakr and Umar RA. Imam Shafi'i once stayed awake all night contemplating 70+ masa'il — and walked to Fajr without needing to renew his wudu because he had never slept. These are exceptions. The exception exists when the ummah demands it. But you cannot make exceptions your routine. Build the Prophetic structure firmly, then when genuine need arises, you flex — and the strength of the structure allows it.

Tahajjud Guide

The Prophet ﷺ regularly prayed 11 rak'at of Qiyam al-Layl as reported in Sahih Bukhari — in segments of two, ending with Witr.

4
1st Set
2 + 2 rak'at with salam between. Long qira'ah. Extended ruku & sujud.
4
2nd Set
2 + 2 rak'at. Continue long recitation. Make dua in sujud.
2
3rd + Witr
2 rak'at with salam. Then 1 Witr rak'ah to end (make it odd).
11
Total
Sahih Bukhari. Ramadan & outside Ramadan. Minimum valid: 2 rak'at + 1 Witr.

📖 Surah Recitation

  • ◆He recited Al-Baqarah, Aal-Imran, An-Nisa across his rak'at
  • ◆At times he would switch the order in the second parts
  • ◆His ruku was equal in length to his qiyam
  • ◆His sujud was equal to his ruku

💫 Signs of Depth

  • ◆His feet would crack, swell and bleed from long standing
  • ◆He had no sins — yet stood this long in gratitude
  • ◆He made abundant dua in ruku and sujud
  • ◆He woke his family to pray alongside him

"O messenger of Allah, you have no sins — why do you stand so long?" He said: "Shouldn't I be a thankful servant?"

Hadith — Prophet Muhammad ﷺ

Tarawih Note

The word Tarawih is specific to Ramadan jama'ah prayer. But the Prophet ﷺ prayed 11 rak'at throughout the year — not just in Ramadan. There is no reason to abandon Qiyam outside of Ramadan. The emphasis is greater in Ramadan, yes. But the routine was year-round.

✅ Always Do

  • ◆Schedule prayers first — work around them
  • ◆Never skip Qailulah if doing Tahajjud

  • ◆Stay awake after Fajr — it is the barakah hour
  • ◆Pray all salawat early, especially Fajr & Dhuhr
  • ◆Make dua in sujud — lengthen it deliberately

❌ Never Do

  • ◆Sleep after Fajr — destroys barakah of the day

  • ◆Doom scroll — especially post-Isha
  • ◆Sleep > 6–7 hrs without genuine medical need
  • ◆Speak excessively after Isha
  • ◆Let family time cancel deen obligations
